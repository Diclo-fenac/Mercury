📊 Trending Algorithm Explanation (Based on Your Database)

Actual Trending Score Calculation:

Formula: (Rating × 0.4) + (Discount × 0.3) + (Availability × 0.2) + (Recency × 0.1)

  • Rating (40%): Product rating (0-5 scale, normalized to 0-1)
  • Discount (30%): Discount percentage from your price.discount_percent field
  • Availability (20%): Total stock across all stores from availability array
  • Recency (10%): Newer products (created_at) get slight boost

Data Sources:

  • Rating: product.rating
  • Discount: product.price.discount_percent
  • Stock: sum(product.availability[].quantity)
  • Date: product.created_at

Caching: Results cached for 30 minutes for performance

Time Period: Configurable (1-30 days, affects recency scoring)

💡 Deals Algorithm Explanation (Based on Your Database)

Deals Detection Logic:

Discount Calculation: Uses your existing price.discount_percent field or calculates: ((price.actual - price.selling) / price.actual) × 100

  • Regular Deals: Products with discount ≥ minimum threshold (default 20%)
  • Flash Deals: High discount (>40%) with urgency scoring
  • Sorting: Highest discount percentage first

Data Sources:

  • Original Price: product.price.actual
  • Current Price: product.price.selling
  • Stored Discount: product.price.discount_percent
  • Stock: sum(product.availability[].quantity)

Caching: Regular deals (15 min), Flash deals (5 min)

Filtering: Only products with actual discounts
